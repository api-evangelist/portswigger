In May 2026, PortSwigger ran Extensibility Month on its Discord server with talks, workshops, and community voting for the 2026 Burp Suite Extension Awards, showcasing how users extend Burp through extensions, Bambdas, and custom scan checks. Over 200 nominations were submitted for 46 extensions across categories including recon, authentication, workflow, and API testing. Winners included Backslash Powered Scanner (Best Recon), JWT Editor (Best Auth), and Highlighter And Extractor (Most Nominated with 18 votes).
